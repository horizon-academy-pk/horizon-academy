Punjab Board Test Preparation

Chapter-wise tests for Punjab Board (BISE) students — built from the board books, following the PECTAA Smart Syllabus (ALP) 2026–27 for the BISE Annual Examination 2027.

How It Works

1. DatabankEvery chapter's questions are collected from the Punjab Board books into a complete databank.
2. Teacher VerificationThe subject teacher verifies the databank — every question and answer is checked.
3. TestA fresh test is set from the verified databank and published here.
4. Checking & ResultsThe subject teacher checks the test, and results are marked and shared.
